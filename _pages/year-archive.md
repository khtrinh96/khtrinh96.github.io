Industrial and Academic experiences
======

PhD in Applied Mathematics and Scientific Computing - University of Rennes
Oct 2023 - Jul 2026, Rennes, France
Research topic: Developed parallelized plasma simulation models using Particle-In-Cell methods in C++ with MPI/OpenMP.
======

* Supervisors: Francis Filbet and Luis-Miguel Rodrigues.
* The project is the first successful simulation in the world of plasma confinement in a torus.
* Developed MPI-parallelized solvers for the Vlasov--Poisson system.
* Implemented Particle-In-Cell and spectral methods for plasma simulations.
* Optimized large-scale PDE simulations using FFT-based domain decomposition techniques. 
* Optimized numerical solvers using OpenMP.
* Adapted MUMPS sparse direct solver for large-scale Poisson systems.
* Applied ghost-point method to the Catersian mesh for the simulation on irregular domains (Circular, D-shaped).
